*diversified portfolio*, *diversification adequacy*.

> “Portfolio asset allocation is a popular topic in finance that indicates the

> “The first method is to diversify across markets, which is to mix a wide variety of
> investments within a portfolio of the cryptocurrency market. The second method is to
> consider the industry sector, which is to avoid investing too much money in any one
> category.”

Origem: `2 parte intro - Cripto/Cryptocurrency trading a comprehensive survey.pdf`, p. 12.

## Fronteira eficiente

**Termos localizados:** *efficient frontier*, *efficiency frontier*, *mean–variance
model*, *trade-off between risk and return*.

> “Tracing the efficient frontier, a continuous curve of mean return and risk
> intersections that indicates the best investment strategy, allows for higher returns
> with the same risk rate. In essence, this model seeks a trade-off between risk and
> return.”

Origem: `2 parte intro - Cripto/Explainable artificial intelligence for crypto asset allocation.pdf`, p. 2.

> “This model forms an efficient frontier, which presents the asset portfolio that
> decreases the total risk under a predetermined expected return. For each level of
> expected return, the efficiency frontier gives the optimal investment strategy.”

Origem: `5 parte intro - estudos relacionados/Portfolio optimization with return prediction using deep learning and machine learning.pdf`, p. 2.

## Observação sobre sinônimos

Os PDFs não apresentaram uma lista formal que declare equivalência absoluta entre
todos os termos. Por isso, os termos acima foram agrupados somente quando aparecem no
mesmo contexto explicativo (por exemplo, fatores comuns ao mercado versus fatores
específicos de um ativo), sem atribuir ao documento uma sinonímia que ele não declara.
